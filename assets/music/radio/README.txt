Radio songs (plan: sound-plan.md section 7.2)

Drop songs here as NN_title.ogg (or .mp3 / .wav):
  01_first_song.ogg, 02_another_song.ogg, 07_rainy_tuesday.ogg, ...

- The number sets the order, which is also the unlock order: the first 3
  songs are there from the start, the rest unlock one by one as Cards
  Organized rises.
- The rest of the name becomes the title shown on the radio:
  07_rainy_tuesday.ogg -> "Rainy Tuesday".
- Stereo OGG Vorbis (quality 6-8) is best; around -16 LUFS.
- While this folder has no songs, the radio plays placeholder tunes made in
  code. They disappear as soon as one real song is here.

Credits: every song must be credited before release. Add a line per song
to CREDITS.csv in this folder (title, artist, licence, source URL), and it
goes in the MUSIC section of the credits roll as its licence requires.
