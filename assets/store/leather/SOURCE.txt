Downloaded 2026-10-21, 1K-JPG. Files renamed: Color -> albedo, NormalGL -> normal, Roughness -> roughness.
albedo.jpg: the colour map times a crease darkening made from the Displacement map (creases down to 55%), then scaled so the raised leather is white.
roughness.jpg: the Roughness map times 1.9 (average about 0.37 -> 0.7), for a matte finish.
Used for: the binder covers and spines (binder.gd LEATHER, shaders/binder_leather.gdshader).

Swapping in another leather: replace the three files, keeping their names.
- albedo.jpg: any colour and brightness. The shader only uses its light and dark relative to its own average (measured when the game starts), so the set's dye still decides the colour; a darker or blotchier texture gives more grain and mottling, not a darker binder. The binder's darkness is binder.gd DYE_DEPTH.
- normal.jpg: an OpenGL-style normal map (ambientCG's NormalGL, not NormalDX).
- roughness.jpg: greyscale, white = matte. Multiplied by the shader's roughness_scale.
Square, seamless, 1K or 2K. Keeping the names keeps the import settings already set up for them (VRAM Compressed, mipmaps, normal.jpg as a normal map); Godot reimports them when the editor next opens. One copy covers LEATHER_SIZE (0.4 m) of binder, and binder.gd LEATHER_RELIEF (3) suits Leather025's faint relief; a texture with deeper relief wants about 1 to 1.5.
