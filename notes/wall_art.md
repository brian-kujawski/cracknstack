**Pickup Wall Art**

While playing Pickup, the player will find certain cards with iconic Magic the Gathering art which will trigger an achievement upon insertion into the correct binder slot. After receiving the achievement, one of the empty picture frames in the room will now hold a large format copy of the art from said card, decorating the space.

This document is intended to track the list of cards which will be used for this purpose, and the progress of the steps needed to implement it, namely:

1) Identify Card

Choose cards with showcase MtG art. We'll probably want around 10-15 of these, but may vary as we see how they fill the space.

2) Obtain Full Art
    
Find a high-resolution copy of the art available for use in the game. This may be more tricky than art for the cards themselves as the art outside of the context of the cards may have different restrictions on use, even non-commercial.

3) Prepare Art

Standardize size, possibly upscale and add a canvas texture to these? Then import into game.

4) Add Achievement Hook

Hook up the text prompt in the game and the achievement functionality.
